CYBERSECURITY • LESSON 4

How to Protect Yourself from Cybersecurity Threats

Casharka 4aad: Sida Looga Gaashaanto Khataraha Amniga Internetka

In Lesson 3, we learned about malware, phishing, social engineering, password attacks and ransomware. Now we will learn practical habits that help protect our accounts, devices and personal information.

Watch Cybersecurity Lesson 4

Watch this video to learn practical ways to protect yourself from cybersecurity threats.

1. Use Strong, Unique Passwords

Create a different password for each important account. Use a long passphrase that is difficult to guess. Avoid your name, birthday, phone number or simple patterns. Never share your password.

Good practice: Use a trusted password manager, where available, to create and store unique passwords securely.
Af-Soomaali: Samee furaha sirta ah oo adag, kana duwan mid kasta oo ka mid ah akoonnadaada. Isticmaal weedh dheer oo aan si fudud loo qiyaasi karin. Ka fogow magacaaga, taariikhda dhalashada iyo lambarka telefoonka. Ha la wadaagin qof kale furaha sirta ah.

2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

2FA adds another security check after your password. Depending on the service, it may use an authenticator app, a security key or a one-time code. Enable it for important accounts. Never give a login code to someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

Af-Soomaali: Xaqiijinta laba-tallaabo (2FA) waxay akoonkaaga ku kordhisaa hubin amni oo dheeraad ah. Waxay noqon kartaa app xaqiijin, furaha amniga ama kood hal-mar ah. U daar akoonnada muhiimka ah. Qof si lama filaan ah kuu soo waca ama kuu soo qora ha siin koodhka xaqiijinta.

3. Recognise Suspicious Links and Messages

Be careful with unexpected messages that create urgency, promise prizes or ask you to confirm account details. Check the sender and website address. Do not open suspicious attachments or enter your password through an unexpected link. Open the official app or website yourself instead.

Remember: A message showing a company logo or your name is not automatically genuine.
Af-Soomaali: Ka taxaddar farriimaha lama filaanka ah ee ku degdejinaya, kuu ballanqaadaya abaalmarin ama kaa codsanaya xogta akoonkaaga. Hubi qofka soo diray iyo cinwaanka website-ka. Ha furin lifaaqyo laga shakiyo, hana gelin furaha sirta ah adigoo raacaya link aanad hubin. Adigu si toos ah u fur app-ka ama website-ka rasmiga ah.

4. Keep Devices and Apps Updated

Install updates through your device's official settings or a trusted app store. Updates often fix security weaknesses. Remove apps you no longer use, review app permissions and avoid unofficial applications from unknown websites.

Af-Soomaali: Cusboonaysii telefoonkaaga, kombiyuutarkaaga iyo apps-ka adigoo adeegsanaya settings-ka rasmiga ah ama dukaanka app-ka ee la aamini karo. Cusboonaysiintu badanaa waxay hagaajisaa daldaloolada amniga. Ka saar apps aanad isticmaalin, dibna u eeg rukhsadahooda. Ka fogow apps aan rasmi ahayn oo laga soo dejiyo website-yo aan la aqoon.

5. Browse and Download Safely

  • Use trusted websites and official app stores.
  • Check website addresses before entering personal or payment information.
  • Do not download files or software from unknown senders.
  • Avoid sensitive account activity on public Wi-Fi; use a trusted connection where possible.
Af-Soomaali: Adeegso website-yo la aamini karo iyo dukaamada rasmiga ah ee apps-ka. Hubi cinwaanka website-ka ka hor intaadan gelin xogtaada gaarka ah ama lacag-bixinta. Ha ka soo dejisan faylal ama barnaamijyo dad aanad aqoon kuu soo diraan. Ka fogow inaad akoonnada muhiimka ah ku isticmaasho Wi-Fi dadweyne.

6. Back Up Important Information

Keep another copy of important documents, photos and work files. Use a trusted cloud service or an external storage device. Protect backup accounts with strong passwords and 2FA. Check occasionally that your backup can be opened.

Af-Soomaali: Hayso nuqul kale oo dukumentiyada, sawirrada iyo faylasha muhiimka ah. Waxaad adeegsan kartaa adeeg kaydin daruureed oo la aamini karo ama qalab kaydin dibadeed. Ku ilaali akoonnada kaydka furaha sirta ah ee adag iyo 2FA. Mararka qaarkood hubi in faylasha kaydsan la furi karo.

7. Protect Your Personal Information

Think before sharing personal details, photos, location or account information online. Review social-media privacy settings. Share information only with people and services that genuinely need it. Log out of shared devices when finished.

Af-Soomaali: Ka fiirso ka hor intaadan internetka ku wadaagin xogtaada gaarka ah, sawirradaada, goobta aad joogto ama xogta akoonkaaga. Dib u eeg dejimaha asturnaanta ee baraha bulshada. Xogta la wadaag oo keliya dadka ama adeegyada dhab ahaantii u baahan. Marka aad isticmaasho qalab dad kale leeyihiin, ka bax akoonkaaga.

8. What to Do If You Suspect an Attack

  1. Stop responding to the suspicious message or caller.
  2. Do not share passwords, one-time codes or payment details.
  3. Use the official service to change a compromised password and secure the account.
  4. Sign out of unfamiliar sessions and enable 2FA.
  5. Contact a trusted adult, your organisation's IT support or the service provider. If money is involved, contact your bank or mobile-money provider through official channels.
Af-Soomaali: Haddii aad ka shakido weerar internet, jooji la xiriirka qofka ama farriinta shakiga leh. Ha bixin furaha sirta ah, koodhka hal-mar ah ama xogta lacag-bixinta. Adeegso app-ka ama website-ka rasmiga ah si aad u beddesho furaha sirta ah una sugto akoonka. Ka bax gelitaannada aanad aqoon, daar 2FA, kadibna la xiriir qof aad ku kalsoon tahay, taageerada IT-ga ama adeeg-bixiyaha rasmiga ah. Haddii lacagtu khusayso, la xiriir bangiga ama adeegga lacagta moobilka adigoo adeegsanaya kanaallada rasmiga ah.

Lesson Summary • Gunaanadka Casharka

Good cybersecurity is built through everyday habits: use unique passwords, enable 2FA, recognise suspicious messages, update devices, download safely, back up important files and protect personal information. No single measure prevents every attack, so combine these practices.

Gunaanad: Amniga internetku wuxuu ku dhismaa caadooyin wanaagsan oo maalinle ah: isticmaal furayaal sir ah oo kala duwan, daar 2FA, garo farriimaha shakiga leh, cusboonaysii qalabka, si ammaan ah wax u soo dejiso, kaydi faylasha muhiimka ah, ilaali xogtaada gaarka ah. Hal tallaabo keliya kama hortagi karto khatar kasta, sidaas darteed isku dar hababkan.
