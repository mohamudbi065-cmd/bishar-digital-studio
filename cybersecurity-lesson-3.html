Types of Cybersecurity Threats

Casharka 3aad: Noocyada Khataraha Amniga Internetka

Learning objective: By the end of this lesson, you should recognize five common cybersecurity threats and understand basic ways to reduce risk.

Ujeeddada casharka: Marka aad casharkan dhammayso, waxaad garan doontaa shan khatarood oo caadi ah oo amniga internetka ah iyo siyaabo aasaasi ah oo khatarta loo yareeyo.

1. What Is a Cybersecurity Threat?

A cybersecurity threat is anything that could harm a device, expose private information, disrupt a service, or allow someone to access an account or system without permission.

Waa maxay khatar amni internet? Waa wax dhaawici kara qalab, faafin kara xog gaar ah, carqaladayn kara adeeg, ama qof u saamixi kara inuu akoon ama nidaam galo isaga oo aan fasax haysan.

2. Five Common Cybersecurity Threats

Threat 1: Malware

English: Malware is software designed to harm a device, spy on activity, steal information, or disrupt normal use. Viruses and spyware are examples. Avoid suspicious downloads, keep your device updated, and use trusted apps.

Soomaali: Malware waa barnaamij waxyeello u geysan kara qalab, la socon kara hawshaada, xadi kara xog, ama carqaladayn kara isticmaalka caadiga ah. Fayrasyada iyo spyware-ku waa tusaalooyin. Ka fogow faylasha aan la hubin, cusboonaysii qalabkaaga, isticmaalna apps lagu kalsoon yahay.

Threat 2: Phishing

English: Phishing is a deceptive email, text, call, or website that tries to trick you into revealing passwords, verification codes, or financial details. Be cautious of urgent messages and check links before opening them. Never share a one-time verification code.

Soomaali: Phishing waa iimayl, farriin, wicitaan ama bog internet oo khiyaano ah oo isku daya inuu kaa helo furaha sirta ah, koodhka xaqiijinta ama xog maaliyadeed. Ka digtoonow farriimaha degdegga kugu cadaadinaya, hubi xiriiriyeyaasha, hana siin qof kale koodhka xaqiijinta ee hal mar la isticmaalo.

Threat 3: Social Engineering

English: Social engineering means deceiving or manipulating a person into sharing information or doing something unsafe. A scammer may pretend to be a trusted worker or relative. Verify unusual requests through a separate, trusted contact method.

Soomaali: Social engineering waa khiyaamaynta qof si uu xog u bixiyo ama u sameeyo wax aan ammaan ahayn. Qof khiyaano wada ayaa iska dhigi kara shaqaale la aamini karo ama qaraabo. Codsiyada aan caadiga ahayn ku xaqiiji hab kale oo aad ku kalsoon tahay.

Threat 4: Password Attacks

English: Attackers may try guessed, reused, or stolen passwords to enter accounts. Use a long, unique password for each important account, enable two-step verification, and never share your password.

Soomaali: Weeraryahannadu waxay isku dayi karaan furayaal sir ah oo la qiyaasay, meelo badan lagu celceliyey, ama la xaday si ay akoonno u galaan. Akoon kasta oo muhiim ah u samee furaha sirta ah oo dheer kana duwan kuwa kale, daar xaqiijinta laba-tallaabo, hana la wadaagin furahaaga.

Threat 5: Ransomware

English: Ransomware is malware that can lock a device or encrypt files and demand payment. Keep separate backups, update your software, and contact a trusted IT professional if you suspect an infection. Paying does not guarantee your files will be restored.

Soomaali: Ransomware waa nooc malware ah oo xiri kara qalab ama sirayn kara faylasha, dabadeedna lacag dalban kara. Kaydi nuqullo ammaan ah oo faylasha muhiimka ah, cusboonaysii barnaamijyada, lana xiriir khabiir IT oo lagu kalsoon yahay haddii aad ka shakido caabuq. Lacag bixintu ma dammaanad qaaddo in faylasha laguu soo celinayo.

3. Simple Ways to Stay Safer

  • Think before clicking links or opening attachments.
  • Keep your phone, computer, browser, and apps updated.
  • Use unique passwords and two-step verification.
  • Back up important files regularly.
  • Ask a trusted person or IT professional if something seems suspicious.

Tallaabooyin fudud oo aad isku ilaalin karto:

  • Ka fiirso ka hor intaadan furin xiriiriye ama lifaaq.
  • Cusboonaysii telefoonka, kombiyuutarka, browser-ka iyo apps-ka.
  • Isticmaal furayaal sir ah oo kala duwan iyo xaqiijinta laba-tallaabo.
  • Si joogto ah u kaydi nuqullo faylasha muhiimka ah.
  • La tasho qof lagu kalsoon yahay haddii wax kuu muuqdaan kuwo shaki leh.

4. Quick Knowledge Check / Isku Tijaabi

  1. What is phishing? / Waa maxay phishing?
    Show answer / Arag jawaabtaA deceptive message or website that tries to steal information. / Waa farriin ama bog khiyaano ah oo isku daya inuu xado xog.
  2. Should you share a verification code with someone who asks for it? / Ma siinaysaa qof ku weydiista koodhka xaqiijinta?
    Show answer / Arag jawaabtaNo. Do not share it. / Maya. Ha la wadaagin.
  3. Name one way to reduce cybersecurity risk. / Sheeg hal hab oo lagu yareeyo khatarta amniga internetka.
    Show answer / Arag jawaabtaUpdate devices, use unique passwords, enable two-step verification, or keep backups. / Cusboonaysii qalabka, isticmaal furayaal kala duwan, daar xaqiijinta laba-tallaabo, ama kaydi nuqullo faylal ah.

5. Lesson Summary / Gunaanadka Casharka

English: Malware, phishing, social engineering, password attacks, and ransomware are common threats. Careful decisions, updated devices, strong unique passwords, two-step verification, and backups can help reduce risk.

Soomaali: Malware, phishing, social engineering, weerarrada furaha sirta ah, iyo ransomware waa khataro caadi ah. Taxaddar, qalab la cusboonaysiiyey, furayaal sir ah oo adag oo kala duwan, xaqiijinta laba-tallaabo, iyo kaydinta faylasha ayaa kaa caawin kara yaraynta khatarta.
