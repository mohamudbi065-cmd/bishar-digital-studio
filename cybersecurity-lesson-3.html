Learning Objectives

  • Explain what a cybersecurity threat is.
  • Recognize five common cybersecurity threats.
  • Identify warning signs of suspicious activity.
  • Apply basic protective measures to your devices and accounts.

1. What Is a Cybersecurity Threat?

A cybersecurity threat is anything that could damage a device, expose private information, interrupt a service, or allow unauthorized access to an account or computer system.

Practical example: You receive an unexpected message asking you to provide your email password. Someone may be attempting to access your account without permission.

Waa maxay khatar amni internet? Waa wax dhaawici kara qalab, faafin kara xog gaar ah, carqaladayn kara adeeg, ama qof u saamixi kara inuu akoon ama nidaam galo isaga oo aan fasax haysan.

2. Five Common Cybersecurity Threats

Threat 1: Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to harm a device, spy on activities, steal information, or disrupt normal computer operations.

Common examples include viruses, spyware, and other harmful programs.

Practical example: A person downloads an application from an untrusted website. The application secretly collects information from the device. This may be a malware infection.

Protection:

  • Download applications from trusted sources.
  • Keep your operating system updated.
  • Avoid suspicious attachments and downloads.

Soomaali: Malware waa barnaamij waxyeello leh oo dhaawici kara qalab, la socon kara hawshaada, xadi kara xog, ama carqaladayn kara isticmaalka kombiyuutarka.

Tusaale ahaan, qof ayaa soo dejisan kara app aan la hubin oo si qarsoodi ah xog uga ururinaya qalabkiisa.

Iska ilaalin: Isticmaal apps lagu kalsoon yahay, cusboonaysii qalabkaaga, kana fogow faylasha aan la hubin.

Threat 2: Phishing

Phishing is a type of online deception in which attackers pretend to be trusted organizations or people to trick users into revealing passwords, verification codes, or financial information.

Practical example: You receive a message claiming that your account will be closed unless you immediately verify your password using an unfamiliar link. This is a warning sign of possible phishing.

Protection:

  • Check the sender carefully.
  • Avoid unfamiliar links.
  • Never share one-time verification codes.
  • Visit official websites directly when checking accounts.

Soomaali: Phishing waa khiyaano internet oo qof iska dhigayo shirkad ama qof lagu kalsoon yahay si uu kaaga helo furaha sirta ah, koodhka xaqiijinta ama xog maaliyadeed.

Tusaale ahaan, waxaad helaysaa farriin sheegaysa in akoonkaaga la xirayo haddii aadan isla markiiba xaqiijin xogtaada.

Iska ilaalin: Hubi qofka farriinta soo diray, iska ilaali xiriiriyeyaasha aan la aqoon, hana la wadaagin koodhka xaqiijinta.

Threat 3: Social Engineering

Social engineering is the manipulation of people into revealing confidential information or performing actions that may compromise security.

Attackers may pretend to be trusted employees, technical support workers, or relatives.

Practical example: Someone calls pretending to be a technical support worker and asks for your account password to solve a problem. You should independently verify their identity and never give them your password.

Protection:

  • Verify unusual requests through another trusted channel.
  • Do not disclose confidential information to strangers.
  • Be cautious of pressure and urgent requests.

Soomaali: Social engineering waa khiyaamaynta ama marin-habaabinta qof si uu u bixiyo xog qarsoodi ah ama u sameeyo wax halis gelin kara amniga.

Tusaale ahaan, qof ayaa iska dhigi kara shaqaale IT ah oo kaa codsanaya furaha sirta ah.

Iska ilaalin: Xaqiiji qofka kula soo xiriiray adigoo adeegsanaya hab kale oo lagu kalsoon yahay.

Threat 4: Password Attacks

Password attacks happen when attackers attempt to gain unauthorized access to accounts by guessing, stealing, or using previously exposed passwords.

Practical example: If you use the same password for your email and several other websites, a password exposed from one service may put your other accounts at risk.

Protection:

  • Use a long, unique password for each important account.
  • Enable two-step verification.
  • Never share your password.
  • Use a reputable password manager if available.

Soomaali: Password attacks waa weerarro lagu isku dayayo in akoon lagu galo iyadoo la qiyaasayo, la xadayo ama la adeegsanayo furayaal sir ah oo hore loo helay.

Tusaale ahaan, haddii aad hal password ku isticmaasho akoonno badan, haddii mid ka mid ah la helo akoonnada kale ayaa halis geli kara.

Iska ilaalin: Isticmaal password dheer oo akoon kasta u gaar ah, daar xaqiijinta laba-tallaabo, hana la wadaagin password-kaaga.

Threat 5: Ransomware

Ransomware is a type of malware that can encrypt files or restrict access to a device and demand payment to restore access.

Practical example: A computer user discovers that important documents cannot be opened because ransomware has encrypted them. A payment demand may appear.

Protection:

  • Keep separate backups of important files.
  • Update your operating system and applications.
  • Avoid suspicious attachments.
  • Contact a trusted IT professional if an infection is suspected.

Paying a ransom does not guarantee that files will be restored.

Soomaali: Ransomware waa nooc malware ah oo sirayn kara faylasha ama xannibi kara gelitaanka qalab, kadibna lacag dalban kara.

Tusaale ahaan, qof ayaa ogaan kara in dukumentiyadiisii muhiimka ahaa aysan furmayn sababtoo ah waxaa siray barnaamij ransomware ah.

Iska ilaalin: Samee kayd ammaan ah oo faylasha muhiimka ah, cusboonaysii barnaamijyada, kana fogow lifaaqyada shakiga leh.

3. Understanding the Difference: Malware vs Ransomware

Malware is the general name for malicious software. Ransomware is one specific type of malware.

Malware Ransomware
A broad category of harmful software. A type of malware that demands payment.
May steal information or disrupt devices. May encrypt files or restrict access.

Malware waa magaca guud ee barnaamijyada waxyeellada leh. Ransomware-na waa nooc gaar ah oo malware ah kaas oo xannibi kara faylasha ama qalabka kadibna lacag dalban kara.

4. Simple Ways to Stay Safer Online

  • Think before clicking links or opening attachments.
  • Keep your phone and computer updated.
  • Use unique passwords and two-step verification.
  • Back up important files regularly.
  • Download software from trusted sources.
  • Ask a trusted IT professional when something seems suspicious.

Tallaabooyin fudud oo aad isku ilaalin karto:

  • Ka fiirso ka hor intaadan furin xiriiriye ama lifaaq.
  • Cusboonaysii telefoonka iyo kombiyuutarka.
  • Isticmaal furayaal sir ah oo kala duwan.
  • Daar xaqiijinta laba-tallaabo.
  • Si joogto ah u kaydi faylasha muhiimka ah.
  • La tasho khabiir IT haddii wax kuu muuqdaan kuwo shaki leh.

6. Lesson Summary

In this lesson, we explored five common cybersecurity threats: malware, phishing, social engineering, password attacks, and ransomware.

Understanding these threats helps you make safer decisions when using the internet, mobile phones, and computers.

Gunaanadka casharka: Waxaan barannay shan khatarood oo caadi ah: Malware, Phishing, Social Engineering, Password Attacks, iyo Ransomware.

Fahamka khatarahan wuxuu kaa caawinayaa inaad si ammaan ah u isticmaasho internetka, telefoonka iyo kombiyuutarka.

Remember: Cybersecurity is not only about technology. It is also about making informed and careful decisions.
