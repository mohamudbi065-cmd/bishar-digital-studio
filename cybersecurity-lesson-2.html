Information Security and the CIA Triad

Casharka 2aad: Amniga Xogta iyo CIA Triad

Information Security waa ilaalinta xogta si looga hortago in la arko, la beddelo ama la waayo iyadoo aan fasax jirin. Waxay muhiim u tahay dadka, ganacsiyada iyo hay'adaha.

1. What Is Information Security?

Amniga xogtu wuxuu ilaaliyaa xogta shakhsiga, furayaasha sirta, diiwaannada ganacsiga iyo xogta kale ee muhiimka ah. Ujeeddadu waa in xogta loo ilaaliyo si ammaan ah oo la isku hallayn karo.

2. The CIA Triad

CIA Triad waa saddex mabda' oo aasaasi u ah amniga xogta: Confidentiality, Integrity iyo Availability. CIA halkan uma jeedo hay'ad sirdoon; waa magacyada saddexdan mabda'.

🔒 Confidentiality — Sir-ilaalinta

Xogta waxaa heli kara oo keliya dadka loo oggol yahay. Tusaale: Akoonkaaga waa in lagu ilaaliyaa furaha sirta ah, si dadka aan fasaxa haysan aysan u arkin xogtaada.

🛡️ Integrity — Saxnaanta Xogta

Xogtu waa inay ahaataa mid sax ah oo aan si aan la oggolayn loo beddelin. Tusaale: Diiwaanka lacag-bixinta waa inuu muujiyaa lacagta saxda ah.

⚡ Availability — Helitaanka

Xogta iyo adeegyada waa inay diyaar ahaadaan marka dadka loo oggol yahay ay u baahdaan. Tusaale: Website muhiim ah waa inuu shaqeeyaa marka macaamiishu u baahan yihiin.

3. Why Are These Three Principles Important?

  • Confidentiality: Waxay ka hortagtaa in xogta ay arkaan dad aan loo oggolayn.
  • Integrity: Waxay ka caawisaa in xogtu sax ahaato.
  • Availability: Waxay hubisaa in xogta iyo adeegyada la heli karo marka loo baahdo.

4. Mini Quiz

Haddii website bangi uu shaqayn waayo oo macaamiishu geli waayaan akoonnadooda, mabda'a CIA Triad ee dhibaatadu si toos ah u taabanayso waa kee?

  • A. Confidentiality
  • B. Integrity
  • C. Availability — Jawaabta saxda ah

5. Lesson Summary

CIA Triad waxay naga caawisaa inaan fahamno saddex arrimood: xogtu inay sir ahaato, inay sax ahaato, iyo in la heli karo marka loo baahdo. Saddexduba waxay door muhiim ah ku leeyihiin ilaalinta xogta iyo nidaamyada.
